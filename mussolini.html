Benito Mussolini

Prime Minister and Duce of Italy (1922–1943)

Home Mussolini Stalin Hitler Mao Franco

Overview

Benito Mussolini (1883–1945) led Italy as head of the National Fascist Party from 1922 until 1943. He created a centralized, authoritarian state, suppressed political opposition, and pursued expansionist foreign policy in Africa and the Mediterranean.

Quick facts

  • Born: 1883 in Predappio, Italy
  • In power: 1922–1943
  • Role: Founder of Italian Fascism; styled himself as the `Duce`

Timeline (high-level)

  • 1919: Founds the Fasci Italiani di Combattimento
  • 1922: March on Rome; becomes Prime Minister
  • 1935–36: Invasion of Ethiopia
  • 1943: Removed from power; executed in 1945
