Joseph Stalin

Leader of the Soviet Union (1924–1953)

Overview

Joseph Stalin (1878–1953) was the Soviet leader who transformed the USSR into a major industrial power through forced collectivization and centralized planning. His rule involved widespread political repression, purges, and a strong security apparatus.

Quick facts

  • Born: 1878 in Gori (now Georgia)
  • In power: 1924–1953
  • Policies: Five-Year Plans, collectivization, political purges

Timeline (high-level)

  • 1924: Lenin dies; Stalin consolidates power
  • 1928–1938: Rapid industrialization and collectivization
  • 1936–38: Great Purge — political repression and show trials
  • 1941–45: Leads USSR during WWII; post-war influence expands
