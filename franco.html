Francisco Franco

Caudillo of Spain (1939–1975)

Overview

Francisco Franco (1892–1975) led Spain as head of state after the Spanish Civil War. His authoritarian regime emphasized stability, conservative order, and national unity, while political dissent was repressed.

Quick facts

  • Born: 1892 in Ferrol, Spain
  • In power: 1939–1975
  • Role: Military leader, head of state, and architect of Francoist Spain

Timeline (high-level)

  • 1936–39: Spanish Civil War; Franco's Nationalist forces win
  • 1939: Establishes dictatorship and rules Spain through a centralized regime
  • 1975: Dies in office; Spain begins transition to democracy
