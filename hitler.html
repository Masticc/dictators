Führer of Nazi Germany (1933–1945)

Overview

Adolf Hitler (1889–1945) led the Nazi Party and as Germany's dictator pursued aggressive expansion, rearmament, and extremist racial policies that culminated in World War II and the Holocaust.

Quick facts

  • Born: 1889 in Braunau am Inn, Austria
  • In power: 1933–1945
  • Ideology: Nazism — totalitarianism, racial doctrine, expansionism

Timeline (high-level)

  • 1933: Appointed Chancellor of Germany
  • 1935–39: Remilitarization and territorial expansion
  • 1939–45: World War II; genocide and mass crimes
