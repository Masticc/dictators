Overview

Mao Zedong (1893–1976) was the founding leader of the People's Republic of China and led the Chinese Communist Party to power in 1949. His rule reshaped Chinese society through land reform, collectivization, and political campaigns such as the Great Leap Forward and the Cultural Revolution.

Quick facts

  • Born: 1893 in Shaoshan, Hunan, China
  • In power: 1949–1976
  • Policies: Marxist-Leninist doctrine adapted to Chinese conditions, mass campaigns, centralized control

Timeline (high-level)

  • 1949: Declares establishment of the People's Republic of China
  • 1958–62: Great Leap Forward leads to economic disruption and famine
  • 1966–76: Cultural Revolution unsettles Chinese society and political elites
